Book of Lies: Mindcrawler

Welcome to the Book of Lies, where dragons ask questions and wrong answers hurt. Literally.

See it in action

About the game

Book of Lies: Mindcrawler is a trivia game wrapped in a dungeon crawler, drawn in retro pixel art. Every enemy you meet attacks with a question. Answer correctly to land a hit. Answer wrong and you lose a life.

Fight your way through each level to the dragon boss waiting at the end. After every battle you pick your next opponent, taking the easy path or the hard one.

Features

  • Trivia battles: right answers defeat enemies, wrong answers cost a life
  • A dragon boss at the end of every level
  • Choose your path: pick an easy or hard opponent after each fight
  • Coins and power-ups: earn coins for correct answers and spend them on hints and extra lives
  • Lots of topics, including history, science, pop culture and mythology
  • Retro pixel art
