About the game

POV Guesser drops you somewhere on an island and shows you the view. Use what you can see, the shape of the hills, the line of the coast and where the snow starts, to work out where you are, then mark your spot on the map.

A new daily challenge arrives every day, so there's always a fresh island to read.

Features

  • Read the landscape: hills, coastline and snow lines are your clues
  • Find your spot on the island map
  • A new daily challenge every day
