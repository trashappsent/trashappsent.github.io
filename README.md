# TrashApps Entertainment

Website for **TrashApps Entertainment**, a small indie team making quirky mobile games.

**Live site:** https://trashappsent.github.io/

## Games

- **Tap Drag Racer**: run the engine by hand and tap your way down the strip. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.trashapps.tapdragracing) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tap-drag-racer/id6820187710)
- **Book of Lies: Mindcrawler**: dragons ask questions, and wrong answers hurt. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.trashapps.bookoflies2) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/book-of-lies-mindcrawler/id6758210945)
- **POV Guesser**: find your spot on the island map from what you can see. Coming soon.
