Tap Drag Racer

No throttle here, you run the engine by hand. Fire each piston in time with the crank and tap your way down the strip.

Watch the trailer

About the game

Tap Drag Racer is a drag racing game where the engine runs on your timing. Each cylinder has a crank needle sweeping toward a target window. Tap the cylinder as its needle passes through, and the engine fires, the revs climb and the car pulls harder.

Keep the rhythm going, shift at the right moment and beat your opponent to the line. Miss your taps and the engine stumbles.

Features

  • Rhythm-based racing: tap cylinders in time to raise RPM and speed, then shift gears
  • 2 to 6 cylinders: Beginner (2–3 cylinders) has wide timing windows, Pro (4) balances power and quick acceleration, and Brutal (5–6) tests your rhythm
  • Timed races against an opponent
  • Keep It Alive: a combo run where you keep the engine going as long as you can
  • Solo practice to learn the timing
  • Local multiplayer over your network
  • Car customization
